# EXCEPTION: HUMAN

A browser rage platformer starring a confidently wrong AI. Friends playtest, October 14, 2026.

Open `index.html` to choose PC or iPhone Safari. `desktop.html` and `mobile.html` are self-contained HTML/CSS/JavaScript game source, with no API keys, libraries, or downloaded assets.

## GitHub Pages

Publish from `main`, repository root. Playtest URL: https://3qinnova.com/exception-human/ (the account Pages site uses this custom domain).

## Controls

PC: A/D or arrows move, Space jumps, X rewrites, R retries. iPhone: landscape Safari, left thumb movement, right thumb Jump/Rewrite. Tap an AI remark to pause and read. The menu offers floor-hint and long-level practice. Some caution tiles bounce you into a Git secret-scanning block; jump over those tiles.

Loss reading lasts 15 seconds; two Continue taps skip it. Compaction deliberately interrupts progress for 15 seconds. Random AI billing dialogs ask for imaginary credits or renewals; choose the free plan and confirm to resume. No real payments are involved. AI billing interrupts at most once per level across retries and compaction. There are fifteen rooms, including nine long levels in the practice selector.

## Audio

Enable Sound (in Menu on iPhone) for an original procedural synth score and 22 layered effects. Music has a separate toggle. Loss reading quiets the music; compaction stutters it and AI billing interrupts with fake elevator music. Backgrounding or pausing stops audio. No audio assets or services are downloaded.

## Scope

The iPhone Safari controls and zoom fixes have been confirmed by the project owner. WebKit checks and an ordinary-input route through all fifteen rooms passed. Android and native iOS builds have not been tested.

The repository and Pages link are public; share the link with the selected friend group. Crawler metadata is not access control.
