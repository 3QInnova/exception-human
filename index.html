EXCEPTION: HUMAN

The AI is confidently wrong. Your job is to survive its corrections.

Play on iPhonePlay on PC

iPhone: Safari, phone sideways, two thumbs. Hold left/right to move; tap Jump or Rewrite X. Tap an AI remark to pause and read it.

PC: A/D or arrows to move, Space to jump, X to rewrite. R retries.

Enable Sound for the new synth soundtrack and effects. Music has its own toggle; iPhone audio controls are in Menu.

Fifteen rooms. Menu includes floor-hint practice and a selector for long levels 7–15, plus a separate Glass sightline test. This is an early browser playtest; other phones have not been tested.

Give it about 10–15 minutes

Try playing without advice first. Losing gives you 15 seconds to read the AI's insult; two Continue taps skip it. The AI may also “compact” your progress. Yes, it is supposed to be annoying.
